# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PitchGenie serves founders, consultants and agencies, and sales or business teams. They arrive with rough business context and need a credible proposal or pitch deck they can refine, export, and share without starting from a blank page.

## Product Purpose

PitchGenie turns an unstructured business brief into a structured, presentation-ready proposal or pitch deck. Success means a user can move from brief to a useful first draft quickly, understand what the system is doing, and confidently edit and export the result.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed, capable, and quietly premium. The product should feel like a calm business-output workspace rather than a chatbot. Copy should be direct, helpful, and specific.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic admin-dashboard composition, glowing AI-startup gradients, excessive glassmorphism, sparkle-led identity, literal genie or lamp metaphors, Canva-like template-marketplace patterns, fake metrics, and controls that appear available but do not work.

## Design Principles

- Make the business output visible: proposals and decks should feel like real documents, not generic cards.
- Reduce blank-page anxiety: each step should explain what to provide and what the system will produce.
- Use calm confidence: clear hierarchy, restrained accents, and useful status feedback beat decoration.
- Keep proposal and pitch-deck workflows distinct while preserving one coherent workspace.
- Earn trust through honest states: loading, failure, limits, saving, and export status should be legible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where practical. Maintain contrast-safe palettes, visible focus rings, keyboard navigation, semantic form labels, non-color-only status indicators, 44px touch targets where practical, and reduced-motion alternatives.
